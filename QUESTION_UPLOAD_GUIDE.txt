QUESTION UPLOAD FLOW
====================

New simplified workflow:
1. Log in as administrator.
2. Create the event.
3. The system automatically opens "Upload Evaluation Questions".
4. Upload either:
   A. TXT - one question per line; or
   B. CSV - use question_upload_template.csv for categories and mixed question types.
5. Review/edit imported questions if needed.
6. Set the event status to Open.
7. Share the evaluation link or QR code.
8. View responses, analytics, and reports.

SIMPLE TXT FORMAT
-----------------
The objectives were clearly presented.
The activity was relevant to my needs.
The facilitator explained the topic clearly.
I can apply what I learned.

All TXT questions use the default type and Likert wording selected on the upload page.

ADVANCED CSV COLUMNS
--------------------
category
question_text
question_type
likert_style
required
options
sort_order

Supported question_type values:
likert
multiple_choice
checkbox
yes_no
dropdown
short_answer
paragraph

Likert styles:
agreement = Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree
quality = Excellent to Poor

Required values:
1 / yes / true = required
0 / no / false = optional

For multiple choice, checkbox, and dropdown questions, separate choices with the | symbol:
Option 1|Option 2|Option 3

You can create the CSV in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets and save/download it as CSV UTF-8.
TXT and CSV uploads are intentionally used because they work reliably on shared hosting such as InfinityFree without extra PHP extensions.

GOOGLE FORM GENERATION
----------------------
After importing the question file, you may open Admin > Google Forms and click
Generate Google Form. The question types are converted as follows:
- likert -> Google Forms multiple-choice 5-to-1 rating labels
- multiple_choice -> multiple choice
- checkbox -> checkboxes
- yes_no -> Yes / No
- dropdown -> dropdown
- short_answer -> short answer
- paragraph -> paragraph response

Google Form responses are linked automatically to Google Sheets in Google Drive.
